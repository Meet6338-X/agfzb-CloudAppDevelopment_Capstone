About Us

Best Cars Dealership - a national car retailer in the U.S. with 50 branches across 21 states.

Our Story

Best Cars Dealership started in 1998 with a single family owned branch in El Paso, Texas. Today we operate 50 branches across the United States and every one of them is run by people who are proud to live in the community they serve.

Our mission is simple: to make buying a car an easy, transparent and enjoyable experience, and to support the communities our dealerships are part of.
